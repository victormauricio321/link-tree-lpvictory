The House

Ten years
one flat top

It started with a borrowed flat top and a line of nine people on the sidewalk. Ten years later it is the same recipe, the same flat top and a much longer line.

We grind the beef every morning. The buns come out of the oven at 10am. Nothing is frozen, nothing waits — that is why the menu is short.
